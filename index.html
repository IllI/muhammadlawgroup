Khaliyq Muhammad

Founder & Managing Attorney

Mr. Khaliyq Muhammad earned his J.D. with certificates in intellectual property and international and comparative law at Chicago-Kent College of Law. He is licensed to practice law in the State of Illinois and holds an M.S. in Intellectual Property Management & Markets from Illinois Institute of Technology.

With extensive experience in intellectual property law, Mr. Muhammad has helped execute trademark, copyright, patent, and domain name transactions across various industries. He has assisted organizations of all sizes in obtaining, defending, and enforcing patents, trademarks, and copyrights. His practice spans intellectual property law, entertainment law, business law, family law, real estate law, and international law.

Before founding Muhammad Law LLC, Mr. Muhammad worked at Intellectual Property Exchange International (IPXI), the world's first financial exchange for licensing and trading intellectual property rights, where he was responsible for valuation and modeling of intellectual property contract offerings, asset pricing, and market analysis.
